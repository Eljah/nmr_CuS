                   CONCLUSIONS
   The relationship between PNMR signal and volu-
metric soil-water content, sensor configuration and
measurement depth can be described by a linear mul-
tiple regression model with interaction terms. The
PNMR signal is a linear function of volumetric soil-
water content and is not affected by clay mineralogy,
soil organic matter content or texture within the ranges
studied. Sensitivity of the measurement to volumetric
soil-water content decreases with increasing measure-
ment depth in both configurations. For any given
measurement depth, the measurement is more sensi-
tive to water content with the sensor in the extended
configuration. The differences in sensitivity due to
